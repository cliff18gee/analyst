About

Clinical insight, structured analysis

I am a Medical Doctor and Healthcare Business Analyst with a strong foundation in clinical practice, analytical problem-solving, data analysis and healthcare operations.

My approach is to translate clinical and operational needs into practical, evidence-based solutions that support informed decisions, improve business processes and strengthen healthcare service delivery. My business analysis skills are developing across requirements elicitation, stakeholder engagement, process mapping, As-Is and To-Be analysis, gap analysis, user stories and impact analysis.

Clinical practice has given me strong skills in critical thinking, interpreting complex information, multidisciplinary collaboration and managing competing priorities in demanding environments. These help me understand stakeholder needs, assess existing processes and contribute to solutions that balance operational requirements with the needs of healthcare professionals and patients.

My goal is to bridge the gap between clinical teams, business stakeholders and technology — combining medical knowledge with structured analysis to support effective change and sustainable improvements in patient outcomes.
