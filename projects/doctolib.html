Doctolib — Improving Online Appointment Management

A business analysis case study on how an online healthcare booking platform could make appointments easier for patients to get and make better use of doctors' available time.

OrganisationDoctolib
FormatBusiness analysis case study
DomainOnline healthcare appointment booking and schedule management
FocusStakeholders, business need, requirements and recommended solution
ContextFirst task of a Healthcare Business Analysis course
ProblemHard-to-get appointments and empty slots
NeedBetter appointment management
Requirements3 functional and 3 business
SolutionUse data on demand and cancellations

01

Company overview

Doctolib is a healthcare company that allows patients to find doctors and book appointments online. It also helps doctors and clinics manage their appointments and schedules.

02

Business problem

Two problems sit on opposite sides of the same appointment book:

Patient side

Some patients may find it difficult to get an appointment at a convenient time.

Provider side

Some appointment slots may be left empty because of cancellations or patients not attending.

03

Stakeholders

Seven stakeholder groups are affected by how appointments are booked, changed and filled:

  1. Patients
  2. Doctors
  3. Nurses
  4. Receptionists
  5. Clinic Managers
  6. Doctolib Staff
  7. IT Team

04

Business need

Doctolib needs to improve the way appointments are managed so that patients can get appointments more easily and doctors can make better use of their available time.

05

Requirements

The need is translated into what the system must do (functional) and what the business must achieve (business).

Functional requirements

  1. Patients should be able to book appointments online.
  2. Patients should be able to cancel or change their appointments.
  3. Doctors and clinics should be able to see their available appointment times.

Business requirements

  1. Reduce waiting time for patients.
  2. Reduce empty appointment slots.
  3. Improve patient satisfaction.

06

Recommended solution

Doctolib could use data to understand when there is high demand for appointments and when there are more cancellations. This information can help doctors and clinics manage their schedules better and make more appointment times available to patients.

  • Identify high-demand periods so availability can be matched to when patients most want appointments.
  • Identify periods with more cancellations so likely empty slots can be anticipated.
  • Share the insight with doctors and clinics so they can manage their schedules and release more appointment times to patients.

This case study was completed as the first task of a Healthcare Business Analysis course. It is a recommendation, and no measured results are claimed.
