POD Orion — AI-Enabled Readmission Decision Support

A human-governed AI-enabled discharge and post-discharge workflow concept for Lakeside Health Network, supporting readmission risk assessment, discharge planning and follow-up prioritisation.

Project typeCollaborative healthcare business analyst hackathon
Dates27–30 September [INSERT YEAR]
ScenarioLakeside Health Network (merger of Lakeside General Hospital and Riverside Specialist Hospital)
My roleHealthcare Business Analyst and hackathon team member

01

Business problem

The merger of two hospitals created operational and data-related challenges that could affect continuity of care, patient identification, risk assessment and timely post-discharge intervention:

  • Different hospital information systems.
  • Inconsistent discharge workflows.
  • Duplicate patient records and different patient identifiers.
  • Inconsistent clinical documentation and data-quality problems.
  • Potentially unreliable risk estimates when underlying data are incomplete or inconsistent.
  • Uncertainty about accountability for AI-generated recommendations.

02

Objective

Design a human-governed AI-enabled workflow that supports earlier identification of readmission risk, more consistent discharge planning, better prioritisation of post-discharge follow-up and stronger clinical governance.

Scope of my contribution. I contributed as a Healthcare Business Analyst within a team: business problem analysis, requirements thinking, stakeholder needs, workflow analysis, identification of data-quality gaps, solution design and consideration of human oversight. I did not independently develop an AI model, and no production system was deployed.

03

Approach

The team applied the following business analysis activities:

  • Business problem identification and analysis.
  • Stakeholder needs and requirements analysis.
  • As-Is and To-Be workflow assessment.
  • Gap analysis across hospital systems and discharge processes.
  • Identification of data-quality and patient-matching risks.
  • Consideration of clinical workflow integration.
  • Proposed AI governance and human oversight requirements.
  • Definition of relevant key performance indicators.
  • Development of practical recommendations.

04

Analysis

Proposed To-Be workflow

  1. Collect and reconcile relevant patient information.
  2. Assess data quality and patient identity.
  3. Generate an AI-estimated 30-day readmission risk, subject to appropriate validation.
  4. Present risk information to authorised healthcare professionals.
  5. Support clinical review and prioritisation of follow-up.
  6. Document decisions, overrides and escalation where appropriate.
  7. Monitor clinical, operational, data-quality and AI performance indicators.

Clinical professionals retain responsibility for decisions affecting patient care. AI provides decision support rather than replacing clinical judgement.

Proposed KPI framework

Clinical

  • 30-day readmission rate
  • Follow-up completion rate
  • Number or proportion of high-risk patients missed

AI performance

  • Sensitivity or recall
  • False-positive rate
  • Model calibration
  • Model drift

Human governance

  • High-risk review rate
  • AI recommendation override rate
  • Time to clinical review
  • Documented override reasons

Data quality

  • Duplicate record rate
  • Patient identity-matching accuracy
  • Missing data rate
  • Record reconciliation time

Operational

  • Discharge process time
  • Staff workload
  • Follow-up response time

These are proposed measures. Baselines, targets and results would need to be established through validation and implementation.

05

Findings

The analysis identified the following issues as the main risks to a safe and effective AI-supported workflow:

  • Systems and workflow gaps: different information systems and inconsistent discharge workflows across the merged organisation.
  • Patient-matching risk: duplicate records and different identifiers could prevent an accurate view of each patient.
  • Data-quality risk: incomplete or inconsistent documentation could make risk estimates unreliable.
  • Governance gap: unclear accountability for AI-generated recommendations.

This was a concept-stage analysis; no quantified findings are claimed.

06

Recommendations

  • Establish consistent patient identification and record reconciliation procedures.
  • Standardise discharge and post-discharge workflows.
  • Integrate AI-estimated risk into an appropriate clinical review process.
  • Maintain human accountability for care decisions.
  • Provide escalation and override mechanisms.
  • Validate clinical safety, performance and fairness before wider implementation.
  • Monitor KPIs and model performance over time.

07

Outcome

The team produced a proposed human-governed, AI-enabled discharge and post-discharge workflow concept for the hackathon scenario, together with requirements thinking, gap analysis, governance considerations, a KPI framework and recommendations. It is a solution concept — not a deployed system.

Business impact

Expected benefits (not measured results)
